Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What we collect

Information you give us: your name, email, phone and messages when you contact us, and your profile, course progress, submissions, quiz scores and event registrations when you use the LMS.

How we use it

To run your programs and LMS account, reply to you, review your work, send updates about your courses, and improve our programs. We don't sell or rent your information.

Services we use

  • Cloudflare Pages hosts the website and may log basic technical data such as IP addresses for security.
  • Supabase stores LMS accounts and data. Passwords are handled by Supabase and never visible to us.
  • Google sign-in (if you use it), Web3Forms for contact-form messages, a scheduling tool for counselling calls, Google Fonts, and WhatsApp (Meta) when you message us. Each follows its own privacy policy.

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies. Your browser stores your login session and preferences such as light or dark mode.

Your choices

You can update your profile in the LMS, and ask us to export or delete your account and data by emailing us.
