Privacy policy

How SkillNavora handles the information you share with us.

Last updated: 23 October 2026

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send: your name, email address and message when you use the contact form, email us or message us on WhatsApp, and any details you share when booking a call.

How we use it

To reply to you, arrange sessions and send information you asked for. We don't sell or rent your information.

Services we use

  • The site is hosted on Cloudflare Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP address for security.
  • Contact form messages may be delivered through Web3Forms, and bookings through a scheduling service such as Calendly or Cal.com, each under its own privacy policy.
  • Fonts load from Google Fonts. WhatsApp links open WhatsApp, which is run by Meta.

This site doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information you've sent us by emailing us.
