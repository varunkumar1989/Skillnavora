## 1. Summary
One paragraph: the problem, the proposal, the expected impact (with a range), and the decision needed.

## 2. Problem and evidence
- **Who:** segment / persona
- **Task and barrier:** what they're trying to do and what gets in the way
- **Evidence:** analytics (numbers, dates), research (n, method), support tickets, reviews — label hypotheses
- **Why now:** strategy link, cost of delay

## 6. Requirements
### User stories (INVEST)
- As a [user], I want [capability] so that [benefit].

### Acceptance criteria (Given / When / Then)
- Given … when … then …
- Include error, empty, loading and offline states.

### Non-functional requirements
Performance (p95 targets) · reliability · security · privacy (no PII in analytics) · accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA) · localisation.
