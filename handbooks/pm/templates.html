Research kit (interview script, persona, usability test plan)

⬇ Download 06-research-kit.md

Customer interview script (45 min)

Consent: purpose, recording permission, anonymisation, right to stop.

  1. Warm-up (5 min): “Tell me about your typical [day/week] when [context].”
  2. Recent story (20 min): “Think about the last time you [did the task]. What was happening before? What did you do first? What else did you consider? Where did it get hard? What did you do next?”
  3. Probe friction (10 min): “You mentioned [X]. What did you expect? How did that feel? What did you do about it?”
  4. Current alternatives (5 min): “How do you handle this today? What does it cost you (time, money, effort)?”
  5. Wrap-up (5 min): “What should I have asked? Who else should I talk to?”

Avoid: pitching; “would you…?”; leading or double-barrelled questions.

Notes sheet: Participant ID · Observation (verbatim) · Interpretation · Journey stage · Tag.

Prioritisation sheet (RICE / WSJF) and Now–Next–Later roadmap

⬇ Download 07-prioritization-and-roadmap.md

RICE sheet

Feature Problem / outcome Reach (unit/period, source) Impact (3/2/1/0.5/0.25) Confidence (100/80/50%) Effort (person-months) RICE Dependencies / risks Notes

RICE = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort. Sensitivity check: recompute with confidence −30 points and effort +50%; if the order flips, treat as a tie.

WSJF sheet

Job User/business value Time criticality Risk reduction / opportunity Cost of Delay Job size WSJF = CoD ÷ size

Now / Next / Later roadmap

Now (in delivery) Next (validated, scoping) Later (ideas)
Outcome 1
Outcome 2
Reliability / tech debt

Rules: outcomes not just features; no dates in “Later”; show confidence and dependencies; review monthly.
