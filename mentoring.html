What you get

Mentoring that ends with action

Every session finishes with notes and the next two or three things to do.

  • Career plan: which PM roles fit your background, and the skills and projects to focus on.
  • Resume and LinkedIn review: rewrite your experience around outcomes and numbers.
  • Portfolio and case study feedback: make your thinking clear and your evidence strong.
  • Mock interviews: product sense, metrics, strategy and behavioural rounds, with a scorecard.
  • AI tools coaching: build a prototype or automation for your portfolio.

FAQ

Mentoring questions

How much does mentoring cost?

Fees depend on the type and number of sessions. See the Programs page or message us for current fees.

Who is mentoring for?

Students, early-career professionals and people switching into product management from engineering, design, sales, operations or other roles.

How long is a session?

Usually 45 to 60 minutes, online.

Can you guarantee a job?

No one honestly can. We focus on what you control: a clear plan, strong evidence of your skills and confident interviews.
