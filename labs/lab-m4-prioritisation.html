1Five Whys: walk down to the root cause

Tap Next. The puddle example shows how to move from symptom to cause. Then try the signup drop.

Problem: a puddle on the floor

Start with a specific problem, not a solution.

Why 1: the overhead pipe is leaking

A cause, not a symptom of the cause.

Why 2: water pressure is too high

Keep asking. Each answer should be a fact or data.

Why 3: a control valve is faulty

Find what is wrong in the system.

Why 4: valves are not tested

Look at the process around the fault.

Root cause: not on the maintenance schedule

A cause the team can change. Fix the schedule, not only the pipe.
