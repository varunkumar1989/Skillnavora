3Handle a conflict, step by step

Tap Next. These are the five steps in the handbook's sequence.

1. Restate each side's interest

Repeat until both agree you understood it.

2. Agree the shared goal

Agree the decision criteria before you debate options.

3. Lay out options with evidence

Use data, research and customer quotes.

4. Decide or escalate together

Share one joint summary if you escalate.

5. Document and move on

Write the decision and the review date.
