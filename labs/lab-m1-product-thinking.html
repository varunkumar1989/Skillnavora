4Breakout walkthrough: Bistro by Blinkit

Five steps, one at a time. Strong installs, but users do not return in 5 to 7 days.

1. Who is the user?

Be specific: a segment with a context, not 'users'.

2. What are they trying to do?

Describe the situation: time of day, budget, mood.

3. Why are they not coming back?

List two or three reasons, each marked as a hypothesis to test.

4. What is the biggest problem?

Pick one reason and explain why it matters most.
