5The strategy case, step by step

Walk through the five steps of the rubric. Each one is a question you must answer.

1. Clarify the goal

Growth, retention, revenue, quality or risk? Pick one.

2. Choose the segment

Who is the sharpest user or buyer to solve for first?

3. Name the trade-off

What will you not do, delay or ignore?

4. Defend the bet

Why this path now? What evidence supports it?

5. Define success

Which leading metric, guardrail and learning signal will you track?
