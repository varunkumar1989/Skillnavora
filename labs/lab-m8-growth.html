2Define the MVP from hypotheses

Tap Next. Each step narrows the MVP to the cheapest test that could prove you wrong.

1. List the hypotheses

What must be true for the product to work?

2. Split them

Problem-market fit (does the problem exist?) and problem-solution fit (does our answer work?).

3. Pick the cheapest test

For each hypothesis, the smallest test that could disprove it.

4. Build only that

Ship the minimum that the tests need, and nothing more.
