Release management (gap closed)

Listed in the course outline, not taught anywhere on the site before this page. Definitions below are general practice, not quoted from any employer.

Release management is the planning and control of when a product change reaches users: scope the release, test it, roll it out in stages, watch the metrics, and roll back if needed.

  1. Scope: agree the release contents and the release date with engineering.
  2. Verify: testing, sign-off, and a go/no-go check against the acceptance criteria.
  3. Roll out: staged release (for example 5%, 25%, 100%) with a feature flag where possible.
  4. Monitor: error rates, latency and the business metrics you set before release.
  5. Recover: a rollback or fix plan written before the release, not after.
  6. Communicate: release notes for users and support, and a status note for stakeholders.

Practice: write a one-page release plan for a change in a product you use, including the rollout stages, the metrics you would watch and the rollback trigger.
