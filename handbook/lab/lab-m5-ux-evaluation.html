4The evaluation method

Tap Next to move through the five steps of a heuristic review.

1. Plan the review

Pick a goal, a flow (for example, menu to cart) and the users you will represent.

2. Walk the flow

Go through the flow like a real user, and note every friction point with evidence (screenshot, step).

3. Score severity

Rate each issue 0 to 4: cosmetic, minor, major, catastrophic, based on frequency and impact.

4. Redesign the fix

Propose one change per issue, and name the heuristic it fixes.

5. Validate before shipping

Run short tasks with 6 to 8 people and watch the live guardrails.
