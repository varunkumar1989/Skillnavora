5Run a discovery step by step

Each step builds on the one before. Tap to move through the Uber pickup case.

1. Who are we designing for?

Four segments: tech-park commuters, mall visitors, airport travellers and first-time riders.

2. Research goal and methods

A survey of 25 people, then 10 one-to-one interviews, then 5 follow-ups after a prototype.

3. Competitor landscape

List what each competitor ships, and the gap that remains for everyday pickups.

4. Root cause

Find the layer under the symptoms: positioning and coordination.

5. Opportunities and metrics

Three opportunities, each with evidence, a risk, and a metric such as first-attempt pickup success.
