## What you need
- A paid ChatGPT plan (Plus or higher). Agent mode is not available on the free plan.
- A LinkedIn account and an Indeed account, both signed in.
- Your current resume as a PDF or Word file.
- 2 to 4 hours. The agent pauses for CAPTCHAs and custom questions, so stay close to your computer.

## Step 1: Map your resume to the right job titles
This tells you which titles and keywords to target, so the agent does not waste applications on poor fits.

1. Start a **new chat** in ChatGPT.
2. Attach your resume.
3. Send this prompt:

```prompt Recruiter map
You are a senior recruiter. Read my resume and list the 20 job titles I am best qualified for. For each title, list the exact keywords and skills that applicant tracking systems (ATS) look for.
```

> If most jobs later score 0 for match, the titles were too broad. Run this prompt again and ask for narrower, more specific titles.

## Step 2: Build one master resume
Stay in the same chat and send:

```prompt Master resume
Rewrite my resume as a master version I can adapt to any role in seconds. Rewrite every bullet with Google's XYZ formula (Accomplished X, as measured by Y, by doing Z) and remove anything a hiring manager would reject at a glance.
```

**What the XYZ formula does:** every bullet becomes "achieved X, measured by Y, by doing Z". "Managed social media" turns into "Grew Instagram from 12K to 180K followers in 9 months by running a daily short-video pipeline". Recruiters skim a resume in about seven seconds, and bullets like this survive the skim.

**What "remove what a hiring manager would reject" strips out:**
- Weak verbs: responsible for, involved in, helped with, worked on.
- Buzzwords: synergy, ninja, rockstar, thought leader.
- Claims with no number: "increased sales significantly" becomes "increased sales 34% quarter on quarter".
- Paragraphs. Use bullets only, one or two lines each.

Save the result. Every tailored version in Step 4 starts from this file.

## Step 3: Turn on agent mode
1. In the same chat, click the **+** button below the message box.
2. Switch on **Agent mode** (some accounts show it as **ChatGPT agent**).
3. Allow it to open a browser if it asks.
4. Sign in to LinkedIn and Indeed in your normal browser first.

> Tip: connect the Indeed app inside ChatGPT (Apps sidebar → Indeed → **Connect**). The agent can then read listings without leaving the chat, which avoids many CAPTCHAs.

## Step 4: Search, score and apply
Send the search prompt:

```prompt Search and score
Search LinkedIn and Indeed for every job posted in the last 7 days that matches my target titles from earlier. Build a spreadsheet with: job link, company, location, match score from 0 to 100, and a resume version tailored to that job. Put LinkedIn "Easy Apply" and Indeed "Apply now" jobs first. List any job that sends me to Workday, Greenhouse or Lever separately as a manual list.
```

A browser window opens that you can watch. The agent searches, filters by date, scores each job against your resume and fills the sheet. This takes 10 to 30 minutes.

**Why Easy Apply first:** one click with your resume pre-filled is where automation works best. Workday, Greenhouse and Lever need an account and custom answers, so keep those for a manual session.

When the sheet is ready, send:

```prompt Auto-apply
Apply to the top 100 matches in the spreadsheet. For each one, use its tailored resume, answer short questions using the job description, and record the result in the sheet. If a cover letter is required, write one under 250 words that sounds like a real person, with no "I am writing to express my interest" opening.
```

A realistic run is 50 to 150 applications over 2 to 4 hours before it hits a rate limit or too many custom questions.

!> The agent applies to real jobs using your real accounts. Watch the window and clear CAPTCHAs and questions when it pauses.

> Tell the agent to skip any job with more than three short-answer questions and log it to the manual list, so one long form does not stall the run.

> Some companies silently reject automated applications. Apply to those by hand.

## Step 5: Raise your interview rate
> **Tailoring beats volume.** The same resume sent 100 times usually gets one or two interviews; a tailored resume per role sent 100 times gets far more (often eight to fifteen). The tailoring does the work, not the automation.

> **Timing matters.** Run it so applications land early in the recruiter's working morning in the target country, not at 3 AM. Applications read first get seen.

## Extra from Vaitis
- **Track replies:** add columns for "Reply", "Interview date" and "Outcome" to the same sheet, and update them daily.
- **Quality check:** before the auto-apply step, open five tailored resumes and read them. If anything is invented (a skill or number you do not have), tell the agent: "Never add skills, tools or numbers that are not in my master resume."
- **Follow up:** five working days after applying, send a short, polite LinkedIn note to the recruiter for your top 10 roles.
- **Practise next:** use the SkillNavora interview questions and practice tests to prepare for the calls you get.
