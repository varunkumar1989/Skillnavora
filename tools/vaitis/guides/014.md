## What you need
- A paid Claude plan (Pro or higher). Connectors aren't on the free plan.
- An Apify account. The free plan includes $5 of usage credit each month.
- The URL of the website you want data from.
- About 10 minutes.

## Step 1: Connect Claude to Apify (one time)
1. Open Claude on desktop or web.
2. Go to **Settings → Connectors**.
3. Search **Apify** and click **Connect**.
4. Sign in to Apify in the tab that opens and click **Authorize**.
5. Back in Claude, Apify shows as an active connector.

Claude can now run Apify scrapers (called "Actors") for you.

## Step 2: Send the URL with this prompt
Open a new chat:

```prompt Scrape a site
I want data from this website: [PASTE URL]. First ask me what data I need. Then: 1) choose the best Apify Actor for the job, 2) configure it, 3) run a small test of 5 to 10 items, 4) show me a sample of the results, 5) if it looks right, run the full scrape, 6) save the results as a clean dataset. Show me readable data, not raw JSON.
```

## Step 3: Answer Claude's questions
Claude asks two or three things in plain English; no settings files or code. For a Shopify store it might ask:
- Which fields: product name, price, images, description, stock?
- How many products: all, or the top 100?
- Any particular category?

## Step 4: Check the test run
Claude first scrapes 5 to 10 items, which protects your Apify credit if something is wrong. You see a preview of the fields, such as name, price, image link and stock.

If it looks right, say **"run the full scrape"**. If a field is missing, say so, for example "also get the review count".

## Step 5: Get your dashboard
```prompt Build a dashboard
Build me a searchable dashboard for this data with a search bar, filters for category and price, sorting by any column, and export to CSV.
```

Claude builds it as an artifact. You can search any item, filter by any scraped field and download everything as CSV or Excel.

## Step 6: Turn it into a paid service (optional)
The same scrape-and-dashboard can be sold:

| Client | What they'd pay for |
|---|---|
| E-commerce brand | Competitor pricing dashboard, updated weekly |
| Real estate agent | Every new listing in their city, filterable |
| Local business | Directory of competitors and their offers |
| D2C founder | Amazon reviews of competitor products |
| Recruiter | Every job posting from target companies |

A typical starting price is $200 to $500 for a one-time scrape and dashboard, plus $50 to $150 a month for weekly refreshes.

!> Don't scrape sites that forbid it. Check the site's `robots.txt` and terms. Public product pages are usually fine; LinkedIn and Facebook are off-limits. Never collect personal data without a lawful reason.

> Don't run huge scrapes on the free tier; the $5 credit goes quickly. Upgrade before a paid client project.

> Always test one page first, even on a site you've scraped before, because pages change. Save the exact prompt you used: the same prompt on the same site gives a repeatable delivery.
