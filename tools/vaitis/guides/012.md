## How it works
The 11 skills teach Claude how to write and manage LinkedIn well. Zapier's MCP connector gives Claude permission to post, and every draft waits for your approval first.

```figure linkedin-flow
```

## What you need
- A paid Claude plan (Pro or higher). Adding skills from GitHub and using connectors needs a paid plan.
- A Zapier account (free to start) to connect LinkedIn.
- A LinkedIn account.
- About 10 minutes.

## Step 1: Add the skills to Claude
1. Go to claude.ai and sign in.
2. Open **Settings → Capabilities → Skills**.
3. Click **Add from GitHub**, paste the link below and click **Add**.

```link Repository link
https://github.com/sergebulaev/linkedin-skills
```

All 11 skills now appear in your Skills list:

| Skill | What it does |
|---|---|
| Post Writer | Writes posts using 21 hook formulas |
| Comment Drafter | Drafts your comments on other people's posts |
| Reply Handler | Handles replies on your own posts |
| Profile Optimizer | Rewrites your headline, About and Featured sections |
| Content Planner | Plans your whole week of posts |
| Humanizer | Removes AI-sounding patterns and checks the draft with several AI detectors |
| Hook Extractor | Pulls the hook formula out of any viral post |
| Repurposer | Turns a tweet, thread or blog into a LinkedIn post |
| Engager Analytics | Shows who liked and commented, grouped by tier |
| Thread Monitor | Flags which of your comments got a reply from the post's author |
| Employee Advocacy | Sets up a team advocacy programme |

> **Skills not showing?** Refresh Claude. If they still don't appear, remove the repository and add it again.

## Step 2: Connect Zapier to Claude
Claude can now write and plan, but it needs Zapier to post.
1. In Claude, open **Settings → Connectors**.
2. Click **Add connector**, search **Zapier** and click **Connect**.
3. Sign in to Zapier (create a free account at zapier.com if needed).

## Step 3: Add LinkedIn inside Zapier
1. In Zapier, open **MCP Servers** in the left menu.
2. Click **Add Apps**, search **LinkedIn**, select it and click **Add**.
3. Sign in to LinkedIn and approve the permissions Zapier asks for.

LinkedIn is now connected to Zapier, and Zapier to Claude.

> **Zapier asks for your LinkedIn login again later?** The token expired. Open **MCP Servers → LinkedIn → Reconnect**.

## Step 4: Use it in plain English
The right skill runs on its own:

| You say | Skill that runs |
|---|---|
| "Write a LinkedIn post about X" | Post Writer |
| "Draft a comment on this post: [URL]" | Comment Drafter |
| "Reply to the comments on my last post" | Reply Handler |
| "Rewrite my LinkedIn headline" | Profile Optimizer |
| "Plan my week of LinkedIn posts" | Content Planner |
| "Humanise this draft" | Humanizer |
| "Break down the hook in this post: [URL]" | Hook Extractor |
| "Turn this tweet into a LinkedIn post" | Repurposer |

Claude always shows you the draft first. You approve it, then it posts through Zapier.

> **Claude drafts but won't publish?** Steps 2 or 3 aren't finished. Check that Zapier shows as connected in Claude and LinkedIn is added inside Zapier MCP.

## Extra from Vaitis
- **Profile first:** run the Profile Optimizer before posting, so new visitors land on a strong headline and About section.
- **Weekly routine:** Monday "Plan my week", daily "Write today's post" and "Humanise this draft", then 15 minutes of comments using the Comment Drafter.
- **Stay authentic:** add a real story, number or opinion of your own to every post. LinkedIn's audience notices generic AI writing.
