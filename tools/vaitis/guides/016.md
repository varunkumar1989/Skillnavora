## How it works
One prompt makes Claude play seven different reviewers who judge your resume independently against one job description. Problems that several reviewers share are the ones to fix first.

```figure resume-panel
```

## What you need
- Your current resume (copied text or the file).
- The full job description of the role you really want.
- A Claude chat at claude.ai.

> Always give the panel a real job description. A resume isn't good or bad on its own; it's good for a specific role, and the panel is much sharper with a target.

## Step 1: Open a fresh chat
Start a new conversation so nothing earlier affects the review.

## Step 2: Fill in and send the panel prompt
Paste the prompt below, replace the two placeholders with your real text, then send. The seven reviewers judge you independently and only meet in the final summary.

```prompt The 7-person hiring panel
Run my resume through a 7-person hiring panel for one specific job.

Rules: each reviewer works independently and does not see the others' verdicts; each judges my resume only against the job description below; be blunt and specific; every point must refer to something actually in my resume, with no generic advice.

For each reviewer give: VERDICT (PASS, BORDERLINE or REJECT), SCORE out of 10, the TOP 3 reasons specific to my resume, and the ONE fix that would most change their mind.

The reviewers:
1. The ATS (automated screener): keyword match, required qualifications, clean formatting. Would it reach a human at all?
2. The recruiter: an 8-second skim for fit, gaps, red flags and level.
3. The hiring manager: interview yes or no, judging impact, relevance to this role and proof over claims.
4. A rival applicant: where would a similar candidate beat me?
5. The CEO: the business case, including outcomes, ownership, leadership and return on investment.
6. A future teammate: would I want to work with this person? Clarity, collaboration, trust.
7. The sceptic: vague claims, buzzwords, unverifiable numbers and filler.

After all 7: A) PANEL SUMMARY with the overall verdict, my average score and the 3 problems most reviewers raised. B) THE REWRITE: a complete improved resume with every issue fixed, stronger bullets, real metrics where I have them (ask me if you need numbers), tighter wording and ATS-friendly formatting. Keep it truthful: never invent experience, titles or results.

=== JOB DESCRIPTION ===
[PASTE THE JOB DESCRIPTION HERE]

=== MY RESUME ===
[PASTE YOUR RESUME HERE]
```

## Step 3: Read the panel report
You get a verdict, score and single biggest fix from each reviewer, then a combined summary.

> Fix the problems several reviewers share first. If 5 of 7 flag the same weakness, that's your most valuable edit.

## Step 4: Use the rewritten resume
The prompt ends with a full rewrite that addresses every issue.

> Be honest with Claude: the rewrite is only as truthful as your input. Never let it invent titles or results. Real numbers win: "Grew revenue 40%" beats "responsible for growth" with every reviewer.

## Step 5: Go further (optional)
Use these one at a time after the first report.

```prompt Go deeper on one reviewer
Reviewer 3 (the hiring manager) rejected me. Go through my resume line by line as that person and show me how to rewrite each weak bullet.
```

```prompt Add real metrics
Here are real numbers from my last job: [details]. Rework my bullets to use them, and flag any claim that still sounds vague.
```

```prompt Tailor to a second job
Here's a different job description: [paste]. Re-run only the ATS and the hiring manager, and tell me what to change so the same resume fits this role.
```

```prompt Final polish
Give me the final resume as clean, copy-ready text with consistent formatting, strong action verbs and no buzzwords. Then list 5 things I should still check myself.
```

> Run the panel for each job, not once. The same resume scores differently for different roles.

## Extra from Vaitis
- **For product roles:** add an 8th reviewer: "A senior product manager judging product sense: problems solved, metrics moved and trade-offs made."
- **Interview prep next:** ask "Based on the hiring manager's concerns, give me the 10 interview questions I'm most likely to get and model answers using my real experience."
- **Combine with guide 001:** use the panel's rewrite as the master resume for the 100-applications run.
