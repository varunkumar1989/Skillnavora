## What you need
- A **wan.video** account (sign in with Google or email; no app or download).
- A clear, front-facing photo of the character you want to animate.
- A short video of yourself, 5 to 15 seconds, doing the movement you want.
- A browser. Free credits are added at sign-up, enough to test a few times.

!> Only animate photos you have the right to use, and never use this to impersonate a real person. Putting someone's face on your movements without consent can cause real harm, and in many countries it is illegal.

## Step 1: Sign in
1. Open wan.video.
2. Click **Sign in** (top right).
3. Use Google or email.

## Step 2: Open Wan Animate
1. On the home page, click **Create** (or **Generate**) in the top menu.
2. In the sidebar, find **Video → Wan Animate** (sometimes called **Animate** or **Move**).
3. Click it. You'll see two upload slots: one for the image, one for the video.

## Step 3: Upload the reference photo
This is the character that will move.

**Works best:** a clear, front-facing photo with the whole face visible, no heavy shadows, head-and-shoulders or half-body, one person only.

**Avoid:** side profiles; faces covered by hands, sunglasses or masks; more than one person; low-resolution or blurry images.

Click the **Image** slot, drag in your photo and wait for the preview.

## Step 4: Record and upload your movement video
Wan copies the motion from this clip.
- Frame yourself from the chest or waist up (the more of you is visible, the more motion it can transfer).
- Face the camera directly, with even lighting and a plain background.
- Keep it to 5 to 15 seconds.
- Do exactly what the character should do: talk, laugh, wave, gesture.

Record on your phone and upload it (or record inside Wan if offered). Click the **Video** slot, upload the clip and wait a few seconds.

## Step 5: Choose output settings and generate
Check the side panel:

| Setting | Choice |
|---|---|
| Resolution | 720p or 1080p (1080p costs more credits but looks better on social media) |
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 for Reels and Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube, 1:1 for square posts |
| Duration | Matches your video by default |
| Mode | "Animate" or "Motion Transfer", not "Replace" or "Relight" unless you want those |

Click **Generate**. A short clip usually takes 1 to 3 minutes. Keep the tab open and watch the progress bar.

## Step 6: Review and download
The preview plays automatically. Check three things:
1. The character still looks like the reference photo.
2. The movements match your video.
3. There's no strange warping around the hands or edges.

If it looks good, click **Download** to save the MP4. If not, improve the photo or video (better lighting, plainer background, simpler movement) and try again.

> The first try is rarely perfect; allow 2 to 3 generations. Simpler movement gives cleaner results; keeping your hands away from your face avoids the most common finger glitches; and a character with body proportions similar to yours avoids stretching.

> Record your movement once and apply it to several photos to keep one character consistent across a series.
