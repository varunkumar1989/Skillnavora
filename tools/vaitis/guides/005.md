## How it works
Ootto is a connector (MCP server) that lets Claude read your Instagram account. Claude studies what already works for you, mixes in this week's trends and drafts a full week of Reels, but nothing is published until you approve it.

## What you need
- A paid Claude plan (Pro or higher). Custom connectors are not on the free plan.
- Your Instagram login, to connect the account through Ootto.
- 5 minutes.

!> Claude writes everything for your Reels and drafts it into Ootto. Animated graphics, voiceover audio and music arrive as **direction** (what to make and the mood), not finished files; you create those in your own tools. Whether Ootto posts automatically depends on your Ootto publishing setting.

## Step 1: Connect Ootto to Claude
1. In Claude, open **Customize → Connectors**.
2. Click **Add Custom Connector**.
3. Name it **Ootto**, paste the URL below into the URL field, and leave the advanced settings empty.

```link Connector URL
https://mcp.ootto.ai/mcp
```

4. Click **Add**, then **Connect**, then **Allow** when Claude asks.
5. A login screen opens. Sign in to Ootto, choose **Instagram** and click **Allow access**.

Ootto now shows in your connectors with an active status.

> **No "Add Custom Connector" button?** You are probably on the free plan; custom connectors need Pro or higher.

> **Ootto won't connect?** Check the URL is exactly `https://mcp.ootto.ai/mcp` and the advanced settings are empty. If the Instagram link fails, sign out of Ootto, sign back in and link Instagram again.

## Step 2: Send the growth-manager prompt
Open a normal Claude chat and paste the whole prompt:

```prompt Instagram growth manager
You are my Instagram growth manager, with access to my account through the Ootto connector. Work through these four stages in order, pause after each one to show me the result, and never publish anything without my approval.

STAGE 1: Study my account
Use Ootto to read my profile and my last 30 to 60 posts. Find: the Reels and posts with the most views, likes, saves and shares; the hooks and first lines that performed best; my average Reel length; my usual posting days and times; and the hashtags and topics in my best posts. Then tell me in plain words my niche, my audience and my top 3 content pillars (the themes that really perform).

STAGE 2: Find what works this week
Search the web for 8 to 10 trending topics, formats and angles in my niche this week. Keep only those that fit my pillars and audience.

STAGE 3: Plan 7 days of Reels
One Reel per day for the next 7 days. For each, give: the day and best posting time (from my own data); the on-screen title hook for the first frame; the first spoken line; the full script, word for word, in my voice; a ready-to-paste caption; 5 to 10 hashtags based on what works for me; visual direction scene by scene; voiceover tone and pace; and the type and mood of music. Write simply and like me, not like a generic influencer.

STAGE 4: Draft into Ootto
Save each Reel's caption and plan to my account as a draft or scheduled post. If Ootto can schedule, set them up but publish nothing until I say "go". If it cannot schedule, just give me everything so I can post myself.

Start with Stage 1 now.
```

## Step 3: Approve each stage
Claude stops after each stage so you can check it:
- **After Stage 1:** a plain-language read of your niche, audience and top 3 pillars.
- **After Stage 2:** a shortlist of trending angles that fit your page.
- **After Stage 3:** 7 complete Reels with hooks, scripts, captions, hashtags, posting times, and visual, voiceover and music direction.
- **After Stage 4:** everything drafted in Ootto, waiting for your approval.

Nothing posts until you say go. Read each Reel, change anything that doesn't sound like you, then approve.

> **It skipped straight to writing?** Say: "Do Stage 1 first. Read my real posts through Ootto before writing anything."

> **Sounds generic?** Say: "Rewrite in my voice. Match the tone of my 3 best-performing Reels."

> **Claude can't see your posts?** Check Ootto shows as connected, then start a fresh chat.

## Step 4: Repeat every week
Paste the same prompt each week. Claude re-reads your latest posts and plans a fresh week.

> If your Ootto account has scheduling on, ask Claude to spread the approved Reels across the week so they post on their own.
