FAQ

Common questions

Is the learning material really free?

Yes. The handbook, AI Tools Lab and case studies are free to read and download. Mentoring is separate; get in touch for details.

Do I need a technical background?

No. The handbook starts from the basics, and the AI tools classes show you how to build prototypes and automations without writing code.

Who is SkillNavora for?

Students, early-career professionals and people switching into product management from engineering, design, sales, operations or other roles.

How does mentoring work?

Sessions are online. Send a message with your background and goal, and we'll suggest a plan: resume and portfolio review, case practice or mock interviews.
