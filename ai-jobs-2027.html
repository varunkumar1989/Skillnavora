Career roadmap · 2027

Get ready for AI jobs in 2027

Pick one AI role, follow a 7-week sprint to the end of 2026, then a quarter-by-quarter plan for 2027. Every step links to free SkillNavora lessons, guides and practice.

47days left in 2026
7weeks to sprint
70hours at 90 minutes a day

This is a learning plan, not a job guarantee. Hiring depends on your market, experience and the roles open at the time.

Step 1

Choose your AI role

Seven roles that AI teams hire for. Pick the one closest to your background; you can switch later. Each track shows the skills employers list, the projects to build and where to learn for free.

🧭 AI Product Manager

Decides which AI features to build, writes the requirements and evaluation plan, and works with engineers to ship and measure them.

Skills employers ask for

  • Product sense and user research
  • PRDs with success metrics
  • How LLMs, RAG and agents work
  • AI evals and guardrails
  • APIs and data basics
  • Prompt and context engineering

Portfolio projects

  • A PRD for an AI feature with an evaluation plan
  • A clickable prototype built with a no-code AI builder
  • A teardown of an AI product: what works, what to fix, how to measure

Your focus for the rest of 2026

  • Finish the AI PM 2 foundations and the APIs lesson
  • Write 2 PRDs from the PRD library ideas, each with metrics and evals
  • Take the PM practice test and the AI product sense questions

Your 2027 plan

  • Apply to APM, PM and AI PM roles with your 2 PRDs and teardown
  • Learn evals and agent design deeply
  • Lead one real AI feature at work or in a freelance project
AI Product Management 2 (free course) →APIs for PMs →PRD library →AI Evals →AI PM job research →PM interview questions →AI Master Course: weeks 1, 5 and 6 →

🛠️ AI Engineer (LLM apps)

Builds products on top of AI models: chat and search over documents (RAG), agents that use tools, evaluations, and the cost and speed work to run them.

Skills employers ask for

  • Python and APIs
  • Embeddings and vector search
  • RAG pipelines
  • Agents, tool calling and MCP
  • Evals and monitoring
  • Cost, latency and deployment

Portfolio projects

  • A RAG chatbot over real documents, with an evaluation set
  • An agent that uses 2 or 3 tools to finish a task
  • A deployed app with logging, rate limits and a cost report

Your focus for the rest of 2026

  • Python for Data Science lessons 1 to 10
  • Generative AI & Agentic AI track
  • Build a small RAG app on free AI (FreeLLMAPI guide)

Your 2027 plan

  • Ship 2 deployed projects on GitHub with READMEs
  • Learn agent orchestration and MCP servers
  • Apply to AI engineer, LLM engineer and applied AI roles
AI Engineer programme →Python track →GenAI & Agentic AI track →How AI agents work →Context engineering →Free AI for coding (guide 25) →AI Master Course: weeks 1 to 4 →

📊 Data Analyst with AI

Answers business questions with data, using SQL, spreadsheets and dashboards, and uses AI to clean data, write queries faster and explain findings.

Skills employers ask for

  • SQL
  • Advanced Excel
  • Power BI or Tableau
  • Python basics
  • AI-assisted analysis
  • Data storytelling

Portfolio projects

  • A sales dashboard built with SQL and Power BI
  • An AI-assisted analysis notebook with a clear recommendation
  • A one-page business case from a public dataset

Your focus for the rest of 2026

  • SQL for Data Analysis lessons 1 to 12
  • Power BI track
  • AI for Data Analysis lessons

Your 2027 plan

  • Publish 3 dashboards with short write-ups
  • Add Python and statistics
  • Apply to data analyst, BI analyst and business analyst roles
SQL track →Excel track →Power BI track →AI for Data Analysis →Live dashboard from a folder (guide 18) →Practice tests →AI Master Course: week 4 evaluation →

🔧 Data Engineer for AI

Builds the pipelines and warehouses that feed analytics and AI, including the document and vector pipelines behind RAG.

Skills employers ask for

  • SQL and Python
  • Pipelines and orchestration (Airflow, dbt)
  • Cloud warehouses (Snowflake, BigQuery, Databricks)
  • Spark and streaming
  • Data quality and governance
  • Embedding and vector pipelines

Portfolio projects

  • A batch ELT pipeline into a cloud warehouse
  • A streaming pipeline with Kafka
  • A document-to-vector pipeline for a RAG app

Your focus for the rest of 2026

  • SQL track to the window-functions lessons
  • Data Engineering & Cloud track lessons 1 to 6
  • Build one small ELT pipeline end to end

Your 2027 plan

  • Finish the streaming and lakehouse projects
  • Add a cloud certification if your target employers ask for it
  • Apply to data engineer and analytics engineer roles
AI-First Data Engineer →Data Engineering & Cloud track →SQL track →Python track →

🤝 Forward Deployed / Solutions Engineer

Works directly with customers to set up and adapt AI products for their business. Half engineer, half consultant.

Skills employers ask for

  • Coding with AI tools
  • APIs and integrations
  • Discovery with customers
  • Clear writing and demos
  • Prototyping fast
  • Security and data basics

Portfolio projects

  • A working integration between an AI model and a business tool
  • A recorded 5-minute customer demo
  • A solution design document for a real company problem

Your focus for the rest of 2026

  • Forward Deployed Engineer lesson
  • Webhooks and automation lessons
  • Practise a demo with the Speaking Coach

Your 2027 plan

  • Build 2 customer-style integrations
  • Practise discovery calls and demos every week
  • Apply to FDE, solutions engineer and AI deployment roles
Forward Deployed Engineer →Webhooks and automation →AI builder tools →Speaking Coach →AI Master Course: week 2 RAG →

⚙️ AI Automation Specialist

Connects apps with AI to remove repetitive work: chatbots, lead follow-up, document processing and reports, often with n8n, Zapier or Make.

Skills employers ask for

  • n8n, Zapier or Make
  • Webhooks and APIs
  • AI agents and prompts
  • WhatsApp and Instagram bots
  • Browser automation
  • Pricing and scoping client work

Portfolio projects

  • A WhatsApp or Instagram auto-reply bot
  • A lead-follow-up automation
  • A scraping-to-dashboard workflow for a small business

Your focus for the rest of 2026

  • Webhooks, triggers and Playwright lesson
  • Browser automation guide
  • Build the SkillNavora-style chatbot from the Vaitis guides

Your 2027 plan

  • Get 2 or 3 small paid projects or freelance clients
  • Package one automation as a repeatable service
  • Apply to automation, RevOps and AI operations roles
Webhooks and automation →Browser automation →Scrape into a dashboard (guide 14) →AI sales agent (guide 3) →Make money with AI →AI Master Course: week 5 project →

🛡️ AI Governance & Responsible AI

Helps companies use AI safely and legally: risk reviews, policies, data privacy, fairness and evaluation of AI systems.

Skills employers ask for

  • AI risk and policy frameworks
  • Data privacy law basics
  • Evaluating AI systems
  • Documentation and audits
  • Stakeholder communication
  • Business consulting

Portfolio projects

  • An AI use policy for a small company
  • A risk assessment of a real AI feature
  • An evaluation report for a chatbot

Your focus for the rest of 2026

  • AI governance guide
  • Governance, Privacy & Business Consulting track
  • AI Evals guide

Your 2027 plan

  • Write 2 case studies on AI risk
  • Learn the main regulations in your target market
  • Apply to AI governance, risk and compliance roles
AI governance →Governance track →AI Evals →AI Master Course: week 4 →

Step 3

Your 2027, quarter by quarter

Q1Jan–Mar 2027

Apply and interview

10 to 15 targeted applications a week, one mock interview a week, finish projects 2 and 3, message 5 people a week in your target companies.

Q2Apr–Jun 2027

Deepen

Go deep on one advanced area for your track (agents and MCP, evals, cloud data or automation). Add one real-world project: freelance, volunteer or at work.

Q3Jul–Sep 2027

Specialise

Choose a domain (fintech, health, e-commerce, education, SaaS) and write two case studies in it. Domain knowledge plus AI skills stands out.

Q4Oct–Dec 2027

Grow

Mentor someone, publish what you learned, and aim for a promotion, a better role or your first paying clients.

Step 4

A weekly job-search routine

Steady weekly numbers beat one big push. Use the guides below to save time on each one.

10–15targeted applications a week, each with a tailored resume
5messages a week to people in your target companies
1mock interview a week, scored and reviewed
1portfolio update or post a week about what you built
