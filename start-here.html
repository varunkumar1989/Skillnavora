Step 2: learn in this order

  1. What is a token? Read the Tokens guide. A token is a small piece of a word that an AI reads.
  2. How does an AI find facts? Read the RAG guide. The AI looks in your documents before it answers.
  3. How do I make an AI do a job again and again? Read the Skills guide. A skill is a saved set of instructions.
  4. How do I automate a task? Read the n8n or Make guide. You connect apps so they work together.
  5. How do I build my own app? Read the Replit guide.

Three rules to remember

  • Use fake data when you practise. Never use a real person's name, email or phone number.
  • Use a test account, not your personal email or work account.
  • Ask a person before an app sends a message or changes something real.
