AI Master Course · Volume 1

From beginner to AI application builder and AI PM

A six-week practical programme: Python and APIs, RAG and vector databases, agents and MCP, evaluation and LLMOps, one deployed capstone, then AI product management and interview practice. Every subject has a plain-English explanation, real code with real outputs, a product perspective, an assignment and interview questions.

Open the clickable handbook Full handbook PDF Back to the AI Jobs 2027 roadmap

Study commitment: 2 to 3 hours a day, 6 days a week, 1 revision day (about 72 to 108 hours). This is a learning plan, not a job guarantee.
