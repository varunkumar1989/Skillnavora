FAQ

Questions about programs

Are the free programs really free?

Yes. The handbook, AI Tools Lab, 12-week plan and case studies are free to read and download, with no sign-up.

Do I need a technical background?

No. The handbook starts from the basics, and the AI tools classes show you how to build prototypes and automations without writing code.

How are 1:1 sessions run?

Online, over a video call. You get notes and next steps after every session.

Can I start with one session?

Yes. Many people start with a single career-planning call or a portfolio review, then decide.
